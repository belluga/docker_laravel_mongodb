## 3. Phase Narratives

The phase narratives articulate the decisive workstreams required to advance the platform through each roadmap phase. Load the specific phase document(s) from `foundation_documentation/system_roadmap_sections/phase_narratives/` according to the initiatives in scope:

| Phase | Focus | Reference |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| P0 – Boilerplate Genesis | Establish landlord/tenant scaffolding, anonymous context handling, and automation foundations. | `foundation_documentation/system_roadmap_sections/phase_narratives/p0-boilerplate-genesis.md` |
| P1 – Capability Baseline | Author reusable identity, anonymous, catalog, learning, and checkout modules. | `foundation_documentation/system_roadmap_sections/phase_narratives/p1-capability-baseline.md` |
| P2 – Experience Orchestration | Align client experiences with backend contracts and observability. | `foundation_documentation/system_roadmap_sections/phase_narratives/p2-experience-orchestration.md` |
| P3 – Commerce and Compliance | Deliver payments, consent, audit, and operational tooling. | `foundation_documentation/system_roadmap_sections/phase_narratives/p3-commerce-and-compliance.md` |
| P4 – Intelligence and Growth | Introduce analytics, personalization, and lifecycle automation. | `foundation_documentation/system_roadmap_sections/phase_narratives/p4-intelligence-and-growth.md` |

When multiple phases are active, review each corresponding document to ensure sequencing and dependencies remain synchronized.
